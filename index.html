Evidence & methodology

The briefing uses dated producer commentary and industry statistics. Each reported paragraph links to its evidence. Reporting periods can precede collection dates; quarterly commentary is not a daily spot-market assessment.

Free, rules-based generation extracts supported facts from recognized primary-source formats. It does not use an AI API. Unrecognized pages create an information gap, rather than an inferred claim. Dated reviewed evidence can be retained during an outage until it expires.
